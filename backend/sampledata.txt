### 6. Rentals & Lifecycle Events
We will create three distinct rental scenarios to populate the timeline/history tables:

**Scenario A (Active Rental): `EX-001`**
- **Rental**: Customer 1 rented `EX-001` for Site 1. Status: `ACTIVE`.
- **Check-Out**: Fleet Manager 1 performed check-out.
- **Site Transfer**: Transferred from Site 1 to Site 2 mid-rental.
- **Usage Data**: 3 days of equipment usage logged (Engine hours: 8, Idle: 2 per day) by `OP-001`.

**Scenario B (Completed Rental): `EX-002`**
- **Rental**: Customer 2 rented `EX-002` for Site 3. Status: `COMPLETED`.
- **Check-Out**: Performed at start date.
- **Check-In**: Performed at end date (Actual return date populated).
- **Maintenance**: Routine oil change performed after the rental completed.

**Scenario C (Active without Transfers): `WL-001`**
- **Rental**: Customer 1 rented `WL-001` for Site 2. Status: `ACTIVE`.
- **Check-Out**: Fleet Manager 2 performed check-out.

### 7. ML Predictions & Notifications
- **Demand Prediction**: High demand predicted for "Excavator" at Site 1 next month.
- **Utilization Prediction**: `BD-001` predicted to have high idle hours.
- **Notifications**: 
  - "New Rental Created" sent to Customer 1.
  - "Machine Transferred" sent to Fleet Manager 2.
